      How to play 3 to 14

      Goal

      Complete each round's goal by laying down every card in your hand as valid sets and/or runs. The goals grow from 3 cards in round 3 to 14 cards in round 14.

      Turn and meld rules

      • On your turn, draw one card from the deck or the top of the discard pile, then discard one card to end your turn.
      • A set has at least three cards of the same rank. A run has at least three consecutive ranks in the same suit.
      • Your whole hand must be covered by sets and/or runs before you can lay down. Cards cannot be used in more than one meld.
      • Jokers and cards matching the round number are wild. Aces can be low or high in a run, but runs do not wrap around. 2s are wild only when enabled for the game.
      • In multiplayer, after someone lays down, each other player who has not laid down gets one final turn. At round end, cards that fit into non-overlapping sets or runs score no points; the game finds the grouping that leaves the fewest points among unmatched cards.

      Tips

      • Keep cards that can complete more than one possible set or run, and watch which ranks and suits are being discarded.
      • Wild cards are flexible; save them for a gap or a difficult-to-complete meld when you can.
      • Before laying down, check that every card in your hand fits into a meld.

      How to win

      In multiplayer, avoid points from cards left in your hand when a round ends. The player with the lowest total score after round 14 wins. In solo play, lay down in every round. The run timer starts with your first draw, pauses while the score board is open, and stops after laying down in round 14. Your score is max(0, 100000 − 1000 × turns − elapsed seconds), so a higher score is better. Leaderboards separate games with 2s wild from games without them.
